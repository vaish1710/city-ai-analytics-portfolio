What this shows

  • With 24 storms of records or fewer, the model beats a hotspot list by about 3 to 12 recall points (12 at 3 storms, 3 at 24). It can use elevation and basin features when the flood history is thin.
  • With the full history, a plain count of past floods ties the model (model minus hotspot is −0.012, about one standard error). Do not expect a model to beat that list on its own.
  • Forecast quality changes how many crews to call out for a storm. It barely changes which segments to rank, so forecast noise did not move these results.
  • Everything here is simulated. A real pilot needs Public Works flood reports and closures, then the same held-out-storm test.
