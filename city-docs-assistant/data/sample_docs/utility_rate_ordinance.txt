Ordinance on Water and Sewer Rates (summary)

[page 1]
Combined water and sewer rates increase by 4.5 percent each year from January 2027 through 2031. Bills are issued every two months. The average household uses 12,000 gallons per bill, and the first-year increase for that household is $4.10 per bill.
The Board held a public hearing on March 9 and approved the ordinance by a vote of 7-2.

[page 2]
A bill assistance program is available to households with income under 200 percent of the federal poverty level. Eligible households receive a 20 percent credit on each bill. Applications are accepted at the utilities office.
