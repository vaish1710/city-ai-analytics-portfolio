Example County Flood Resilience Plan (excerpt)

[page 1]
About 9 percent of the county's land area lies in the special flood hazard area, which includes roughly 3,100 parcels. The plan sets the design standard for new drainage at the 10-year, 24-hour storm, which is 5.6 inches of rain.

[page 2]
The Harbor Road elevation project raises 1.4 miles of roadway so emergency vehicles can reach the regional hospital during floods. The cost estimate is $22 million. A state grant pays for 60 percent of the project.
The home elevation program aims to raise 250 homes over five years. The first group of 40 homes will be chosen by the end of the year.
